Zero-K / Spring smoke components

Game: Zero-K, commit ee8950e2c9af13dfe5273df1348a4be0d2bd0b90

Textures imported from the game's bitmaps/PD directory:
SmokeTrail.tga (trail.png), SmokeSmall.tga (smoke_small.png),
smoke1.tga (smoke.png; also repeated in clouds.png).
Upstream classifies these assets as PD (public domain).
TGA decoded to PNG, unchanged RGBA pixels. The material applies a white-grey
tint and stronger opacity at runtime. No proprietary War Thunder assets used.
